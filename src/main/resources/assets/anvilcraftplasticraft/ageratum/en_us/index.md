# Anvilcraft: Plasticraft

Welcome to the plastic works. Inside you will find resin gear that bounces, a tower that splits crude oil into three fluids, four plastic materials with very different personalities, an editable molding system, and a crew of hard-hatted allays that build for you

For a first playthrough, follow [From Resin to Plastic](000_process/001.md) — five steps and you have the backbone. After that each branch stands on its own and can be used in any order, as soon as its materials are available
