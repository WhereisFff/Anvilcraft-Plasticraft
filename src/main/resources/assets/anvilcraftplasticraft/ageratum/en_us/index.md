# Anvilcraft: Plasticraft

This addon extends AnvilCraft with resin products, crude-oil fractionation, four plastic materials, editable molding, and blueprint construction by allays

For a first playthrough, follow [From Resin to Plastic](000_process/001.md). Each branch can also be used independently once its materials are available
