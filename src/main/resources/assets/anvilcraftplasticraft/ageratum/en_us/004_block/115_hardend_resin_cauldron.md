# Hardened Resin Cauldron

<recipe id="anvilcraftplasticraft:hardend_resin_cauldron"/>

<recipe id="anvilcraftplasticraft:magnetic_hardend_resin_cauldron"/>

The hardened resin cauldron is a movable entity affected by gravity and buoyancy, and it can attach to all six faces. It stores items and up to one bucket of fluid, participates in AnvilCraft cauldron recipes, and exposes its contents to Jade.

- The ordinary recipe follows the vanilla cauldron shape with all seven iron ingots replaced by hardened resin.
- The magnetic recipe replaces the two top hardened-resin ingredients with magnet ingots and participates in magnetic force and acceleration mechanics.
- Empty-hand use retrieves stored items. Normal fluid containers insert or extract arbitrary supported amounts.
- Sneak-use it with any Anvil Hammer to retrieve the cauldron and its stored items directly into the inventory.
- Any side can connect to a pipe head, pump, or control valve. The tank uses normal container transfers rather than whole-cauldron layers.
- Creative players can Shift-use a magnet or magnet-mode multitool to magnetize the cauldron.
- A full non-magnetic cauldron spills through a side- or downward-facing opening; a magnetic cauldron retains its fluid.
- The body uses cutout rendering so translucent fluid does not erase the submerged inner walls.
