# Plastic Anvil

<recipe id="anvilcraftplasticraft:plastic_anvil"/>

# Function

- Hold <ref item="anvilcraftplasticraft:plastic_anvil"/> and right-click the ground to place a solid plastic anvil entity.
- The entity has a full-block hard collision box and can be pushed by players and other pushable entities. It does not behave like a falling block.
- Right-click the placed entity to open the normal Royal Anvil repair menu.
- The menu supports the usual vanilla anvil repair, rename, and enchantment combination workflow. The entity is not damaged when the menu is used.
- The item stores a plastic color for future variants. The model and Jade information are ready to display that color.

The current snowball recipe is temporary. More involved plastic processing recipes will replace it in a later update.
