# From Resin to Plastic

<tip>
You do not need every branch at once: resin anvils and high-viscosity resin work before the plastic line is complete
</tip>

## 1. Start with resin products

- Craft a <ref item="anvilcraftplasticraft:resin_anvil"/> from resin blocks and resin
- Fast-cook the resin anvil in a dry cauldron to obtain a hardened, non-bouncing anvil
- Combine a resin anvil, a lightning rod and hardened resin for the <ref item="anvilcraftplasticraft:resin_anvil_hammer"/>
- [Resin products](../001_resin/index.md)

## 2. Fractionate crude oil

- Build the [Condenser Tower](../002_refining/000_condenser_tower.md) from its multiblock recipe
- Plasma jets turn the top fluid in a Large Cauldron into an equal amount of gas; crude oil, water and experience fluid are supported
- The first three tower levels condense crude-oil gas into high-heat fuel, plastic oil and crude-oil essence

## 3. Choose a melt

- With royal steel or frost metal present in Plastic Oil, heat the fluid from below for Universal Plastic Melt
- Use royal glass or frost glass instead for clear plastic melt
- Expose universal plastic melt to royal steel, frost metal or ember metal for engineering or heat-resistant plastic melt
- [Plastic melts](../002_refining/010_plastic_melts.md)

## 4. Cool and mold

- Water, powder snow or a cold item turns 1000mB of melt into 16 plastic granules
- A world melt source solidifies in place when it meets rain, adjacent water or a cooling block
- [Plastic materials](../003_plastic/000_material_comparison.md) explains color and material differences; [the molding system](../004_molding/index.md) explains production

## 5. Automate construction

- Import a blueprint into a structure disk and deploy its projection
- Put a plastic hard hat on an allay, then use an [Allay Lounge](../005_construction/010_allay_lounge.md) to provide the disk and materials
- [Construction jobs](../005_construction/030_construction_jobs.md) covers demolition, collection, building and cancellation
