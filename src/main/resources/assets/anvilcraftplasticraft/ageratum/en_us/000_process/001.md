# From Resin to Plastic

<tip>
You do not need every branch at once: resin anvils and high-viscosity resin are complete toys on their own, so feel free to start there
</tip>

## 1. Start with resin products

- Craft a <ref item="anvilcraftplasticraft:resin_anvil"/> from resin blocks and resin — an anvil that bounces
- Fast-cook it in a dry cauldron and the bounce is gone, leaving a hardened resin anvil
- Combine a resin anvil, a lightning rod and hardened resin for the <ref item="anvilcraftplasticraft:resin_anvil_hammer"/>
- More to play with in [resin products](../001_resin/index.md)

## 2. Fractionate crude oil

- Build the [Condenser Tower](../002_refining/000_condenser_tower.md) from its multiblock recipe, right on top of a Large Cauldron
- Plasma jets do the boiling: they turn the top fluid in the cauldron into an equal amount of gas, and crude oil, water and experience fluid all work
- Crude-oil gas climbs on its own, and the first three tower levels condense it into high-heat fuel, plastic oil and crude-oil essence

## 3. Choose a melt

- With royal steel or frost metal present in Plastic Oil, heat the fluid from below for Universal Plastic Melt
- Use royal glass or frost glass instead for clear plastic melt
- Expose universal plastic melt to royal steel, frost metal or ember metal to keep going toward engineering or heat-resistant plastic melt
- What each of the four routes needs is in [plastic melts](../002_refining/010_plastic_melts.md)

## 4. Cool and mold

- Water, powder snow or a cold item turns 1000mB of melt into 16 plastic granules
- A world melt source solidifies on the spot when it meets rain, adjacent water or a cooling block
- [Plastic materials](../003_plastic/000_material_comparison.md) explains color and material differences; [the molding system](../004_molding/index.md) explains production

## 5. Automate construction

- Import a blueprint into a structure disk and deploy its projection where you want to build
- Put a plastic hard hat on an allay, then let an [Allay Lounge](../005_construction/010_allay_lounge.md) hand out the disk and materials
- [Construction jobs](../005_construction/030_construction_jobs.md) covers how demolition, collection, building and cancellation each handle resources
