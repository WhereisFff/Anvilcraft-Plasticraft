Plastic Melt left to cool becomes a block; sent through [Plastic Molding](../004_molding/index.md) it becomes a crate, tank, cauldron, anvil, or tray. All five materials share molding and placement rules, with Elastic Plastic also bouncing on collision. Material differences are collected in [Material Comparison](000_material_comparison.md)
