# Trays and Allay Hard Hats

## Redstone tray

A tray model must fit inside the 48x48px modeling area, be no more than 4px tall, and have a flat solid surface at both its lowest and highest points. Its nine fixed 16x16px cells are mounting positions; each cell holds one component, up to 9 per tray

Supported components include Redstone Repeaters, Comparators, Redstone Torches, Buttons, Levers, Pressure Plates, Daylight Detectors, and AnvilCraft Advanced Comparators, Pulse Generators and Item Detectors. Use a component on a supported empty cell to place it; sneak-use an empty hand on an occupied cell to retrieve it

- Components keep their vanilla model, menu, delay, interaction and redstone state
- Adjacent cells can pass signals inside the tray; external input and output follow the tray's position and orientation
- Moving, rotating or bonding a tray moves its components and their states together

## Allay Hard Hat

Choose the Allay Hard Hat type in the molding chamber. The model may be at most 11x11px across and 16px tall. Because this type must be printed, it must also fit inside the central 32x32x32px printing region and requires a [3D Printing Component](../004_molding/000_molding_chamber.md). Right-click a vanilla Allay with the finished product to turn it into a Working Allay; sneak-right-click to remove it

- Universal and Clear Plastic Hard Hats have no extra ability
- An Engineering Plastic Hard Hat gives a demolition allay Silk Touch while it holds a Stonecutter
- A Heat-Resistant Plastic Hard Hat grants fire immunity and Smelting demolition

See [Working Allay](../005_construction/000_working_allay.md) for tools, material supply and jobs
