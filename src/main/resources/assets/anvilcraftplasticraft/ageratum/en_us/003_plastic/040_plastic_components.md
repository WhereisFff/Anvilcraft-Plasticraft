# Redstone Trays

## Redstone tray

A tray is a thin plate: the model must fit inside the 48x48px modeling area, stay no more than 4px tall, and have a flat solid surface at both its lowest and highest points. Its nine fixed 16x16px cells are the mounting positions — one component per cell, up to 9 per tray

There is plenty to mount: Redstone Repeaters, Comparators, Redstone Torches, Buttons, Levers, Pressure Plates, Daylight Detectors, and AnvilCraft Advanced Comparators, Pulse Generators and Item Detectors. Use a component on a supported empty cell to place it; sneak-use an empty hand on an occupied cell to take it back

- Components keep their vanilla model, menu, delay, interaction and redstone state, so everything you already know still applies
- Adjacent cells can pass signals inside the tray, while external input and output follow the tray's position and orientation
- Moving, rotating or bonding a tray takes its components and their states along
